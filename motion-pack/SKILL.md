# motion-pack — Router

10 drop-in motion-graphics templates (HTML/CSS/GSAP) + access to ~115 across HyperFrames.

## How to use
1. Pick a template from the table below (or browse `CATALOG.md` for all ~115).
2. Follow `PIPELINE.md` to render it (fill the prompt, screenshot/record, export).
3. Templates live in `templates/`; render helper is `render-scene.js`.
